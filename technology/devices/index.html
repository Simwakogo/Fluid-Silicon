    Showing all 6 families.

    Two layers, so your view never changes.

    What you see and act on is the same on every vendor. What differs between vendors is handled by Fluid Silicon.

    Vendor-neutral, design-agnostic

    What your team works with

    • Health metric: measured slack as a share of the clock period, per logic-element link
    • Telemetry schema
    • Alert thresholds
    • Aging model and time-to-threshold

    Vendor-specific

    What Fluid Silicon handles

    • Integration with each vendor's toolchain, including AMD Vivado and Altera Quartus
    • Calibration for each family and process node
    • Qualification of new families before they are marked supported

    On the roadmap.

    Newer nodes and RF-sampling parts are in development.

    • Altera Stratix 10 and Arria 10 (14 nm and 20 nm)
    • AMD Versal AI Core (7 nm)
    • RF-sampling adaptive SoCs for aerospace and defense, such as AMD Versal RF
