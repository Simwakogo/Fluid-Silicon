Intern Positions

Paid. Real scope, real hardware, a named owner for your project.

About Fluid Silicon

Fluid Silicon builds the platform that lets deployed chips monitor their own health, adapt to their own silicon, and repair themselves in the field. The company was founded out of reconfigurable computing research at the University of Pennsylvania and was awarded the Penn President's Sustainability Prize. We work on commercial FPGAs, in real deployments, on problems most of the industry treats as fixed at fabrication.

The Program

Interns at Fluid Silicon get one defined project, one named mentor, and real hardware. You will present your results to the whole team at the end of the internship, and strong performance leads to a return or full-time offer. All positions are paid and based in Philadelphia.

Software Engineering Intern

Ship a real piece of the platform: telemetry visualization, device test automation, or a component of the runtime that talks to live chips. You will write code that outlasts your internship.

  • Strong fundamentals in Python and at least one of C, C++, or Rust
  • Coursework or projects in operating systems, computer architecture, or embedded systems
  • Curiosity about what happens below the software you write
Apply: SWE Intern

Hardware Design / FPGA Intern

Own a bounded RTL block or a silicon characterization experiment end to end: design it, simulate it, run it on a board, and present the measured data. Few internships put you this close to real silicon.

  • Coursework or projects in digital design with Verilog, SystemVerilog, or VHDL
  • Exposure to an FPGA toolchain (Vivado or Quartus) in class or on your own
  • Comfort being wrong in simulation and finding out why on hardware
Apply: HDE/FPGA Intern

Business Development Intern

Work directly with the founder on go-to-market: map target accounts in defense, cloud, and finance, prepare material for pilot conversations, and sit in on the conversations themselves.

  • Clear, direct writing
  • Comfort learning deep technology quickly and explaining it simply
  • Interest in how hardware companies find their first customers
Apply: BD Intern

How to Apply

Email careers@fluidsilicon.com with the role in the subject line, your resume, and 3 to 5 sentences on the most interesting thing you have built or figured out. No cover letters.
