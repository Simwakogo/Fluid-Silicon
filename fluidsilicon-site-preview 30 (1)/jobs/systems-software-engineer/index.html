Systems Software Engineer, HW/SW Co-Design

About Fluid Silicon

Fluid Silicon builds critical infrastructure for reconfigurable computing to advance efficiency and reliability. Innovation is in our DNA.

The Role

Whether you are interested in embedded platforms, hardware-software codesign, system software, this role is the right fit for you. As a system software engineer you will have the chance to build firmware, software, and hardware designs that will be used by millions of devices.

We are looking for humble, collaborative, and highly motivated individuals to join our team and meaningfully contribute to our designs.

Responsibilities

  • Build from basic to sophisticate software systems that are robust, reliable, and scalable
  • Write drivers and low-level interfaces to FPGA platforms and embedded Linux
  • Design scheduling policies and resource management strategies for millions of components

Minimum Qualifications

  • BS or MS in computer science, computer engineering, or equivalent experience
  • 3+ years of systems programming in C, C++, Python, or Rust
  • Experience programming against real hardware: drivers, memory-mapped I/O, DMA, embedded Linux
  • Enough digital design literacy to read RTL and negotiate an interface with the people who write it
  • Strong judgment on reliability, observability, and failure modes in long-running systems

Preferred Qualifications

  • FPGA runtime experience such as XRT or OPAE
  • Production telemetry, fleet management, or predictive maintenance systems
  • Performance analysis and modeling of hardware/software systems
  • Contributions to open-source systems or tooling
