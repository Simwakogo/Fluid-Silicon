    Working together

    A small team, one product, built on trust.

    We are building a product whose whole promise is that hardware can be trusted, so we hold our own work to the same standard: measure before claiming, say the true thing early, and put the customer's problem first. Everyone owns real parts of the product, and the next hires will shape how we work as much as we do.

    How we hire

    A short process, with a person reading every application.

    1. Apply

      A résumé and links to work you're proud of. We reply within five business days.

    2. Intro call

      Thirty minutes on the role, the team and what you want to work on.

    3. Technical conversation

      A working session on a real problem from our domain.

    4. Meet the team

      Time with the people you'd work with. We decide quickly after that.
