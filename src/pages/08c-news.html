About Fluid Silicon

Fluid Silicon builds FPGA health monitoring and timing measurement software. Its platform measures the timing of every logic element on a chip while the chip runs, so the people who operate critical systems can see what each chip can really do, run it at its real limits and know when it will need attention. The platform works today on AMD and Altera FPGAs and grew out of reconfigurable-computing research at the University of Pennsylvania, which awarded founder Nhlanhla Mavuso its 2026 President's Sustainability Prize for the work. Fluid Silicon is based in Philadelphia, PA.
