    One measurement, read differently in every industry.

    Fluid Silicon matters most where chips run for years, in large numbers or out of reach, and where a surprise failure is expensive. Each industry reads the same measurement as something different: watts, uptime, a maintenance schedule, evidence.

    Board & System MakersData Center & CloudAI / HPCAerospace & DefenseTelecommunicationsTest & Measurement

    Board & System Makers

    Every card you ship carries the vendor's worst-case margin, because until now there was no way to measure each one. Characterize every card before it leaves, show what it sustains, and give customers a health signal for the life of the board.
